# English writing standards

Write new documentation and interface text in English unless the project owner requests another language. Preserve source quotations in their original language.

This document is the canonical writing policy for this repository. Skills refer here instead of copying these rules.

Clear writing also helps agents. When terms stay stable and claims stay labeled, models make fewer invented details.

## Apply these standards together

- **Google Developer Documentation Style Guide:** Use active voice, second person for instructions, sentence-case headings, descriptive links, and clear dates. Use numbered lists for sequences. Keep the tone friendly and respectful.
- **ASD-STE100-derived precision:** Use one term for each concept, simple verb forms, and explicit actors. Put conditions before instructions. State one action per instruction. Limit procedural sentences to 20 words and descriptive sentences to 25 words. Preserve complete grammar.
- **Zinsser's four principles:** Make the meaning clear, choose simple words, remove unnecessary text, and write with humanity. Give readers useful context without condescension or forced enthusiasm.

## Structure and labels

Lead with the result or action the reader needs. Keep related ideas together. Use tables for comparisons and responsibility assignments.

Separate evidence, interpretation, proposals, and decisions. Distinguish recorded owners and deadlines from proposed assignments. Label proposed assignments explicitly.

Use concrete descriptions instead of hype, vague promises, or filler. Explain what a control does and where its result goes.

Preserve code, commands, identifiers, file paths, product names, and exact quotations. Sentence limits do not justify changing them.

## Before delivery

1. Check the longest sentences against the limits above.
2. Check terminology, actors, conditions, dates, and evidence labels.
3. Read the text aloud or review its natural flow.
4. Remove repetition and words that do not help the reader act or understand.

## Compliance note

These are derived writing rules, not a claim of certified ASD-STE100 compliance. Full compliance requires the official rules and dictionary.
